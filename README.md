# Elena Gostiukhina

I am a Data Science and Artificial Intelligence student at Maastricht University, interested in how rigorous data analysis, predictive modelling, and thoughtful software design can inform practical decisions.

I enjoy investigating complex datasets, identifying unexpected patterns, and developing models that address a clearly defined problem. My approach combines experimentation with an emphasis on interpretability: understanding why a model behaves as it does and communicating its results in a way that is meaningful to the people using them.

## Areas of Focus

- **Exploratory analysis and feature engineering:** investigating relationships, anomalies, and the assumptions underlying the data.
- **Machine learning and ensemble methods:** developing, tuning, and evaluating models with attention to the demands of the task.
- **Explainable AI:** examining the factors behind predictions and making model behaviour easier to assess.
- **Analytical applications:** designing intuitive dashboards and interfaces that reduce the effort required to understand complex results.

My broader interests include healthcare AI, natural language processing, recommender systems, cloud engineering, and software engineering.

## Selected Projects

### [Cardiovascular Data Pipeline](https://github.com/elenagos/cardiovascular-data-pipeline)

A system for streaming, storing, and analysing simulated cardiovascular measurements. The project connects data validation and feature engineering with anomaly detection, an API, and an interactive dashboard.

**Technologies:** Python, Java, PostgreSQL, FastAPI, Streamlit, Docker.

### [Acute Kidney Injury Risk Prediction](https://github.com/elenagos/aki-risk-prediction)

A collaborative research project investigating acute kidney injury risk in ICU patients using MIMIC-IV clinical data. My contributions focused on clinical feature engineering, missing-data handling, class imbalance, Logistic Regression and XGBoost optimisation, and SHAP-based interpretation.

**Technologies:** Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP, Streamlit.

**Publication:** Coauthor of [*Early Prediction of Acute Kidney Injury Risk after Nephrotoxic Antibiotic Exposure*](https://openreview.net/forum?id=WiDXeSmAhr), presented as a poster at BNAIC/BeNeLearn 2026.
